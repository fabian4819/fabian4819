### About me

I'm a full stack developer from Indonesia and an Information Technology graduate of **Universitas Gadjah Mada**. I like building systems that go all the way to production and stay there: a ticketing platform that survives flash sales, an ERP used across three mining sites, and WhatsApp automation that agencies use every day.

Most of my work lives on the backend: Go microservices, Kafka, Redis, PostgreSQL, Docker and AWS, with CI/CD wired up in GitHub Actions. I'm just as comfortable shipping the frontend or a mobile app when the product needs it.

- 🎫 Building **[Roetix](https://roetix.com)**, an event ticketing platform built from the ground up (Vite + 9 Go microservices)
- 🗺️ Working on **[LAM.ID](https://lamid.ptvi.co.id)**, a land management ERP for PT Vale Indonesia, and its mobile survey app **[LAMIND](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lamind/id6791062554)**
- 🤖 Building WhatsApp automation and **[AZERAKOL.ID](https://azerakol.id)**, a SaaS campaign tracker for KOL agencies
- ⛓️ Exploring DeFi, on-chain agents and real-world asset tokenization
